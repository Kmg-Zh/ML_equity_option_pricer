## What was built

| Comparison | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| Surrogate AD vs Surrogate FD | Internal sanity — should be small |

| Surrogate FD vs Teacher FD | Consistency — pricing-level error propagated into FD |

## Root cause analysis — Vega error

The large Vega error has at least three contributing factors:

1. **Surrogate pricing error in sigma**: If the surrogate price at `sigma + δv` and `sigma - δv` is off even by ~0.3%, a delta-Vega of ~15 is implied at σ=20%, T=2y. The surrogate MAE is ~1.5% of price in long-tenor buckets.

2. **AD chain-rule limitation**: The surrogate's `∂z/∂σ_norm` is the gradient of a piecewise-linear function — it is constant on ReLU linear regions and undefined at kinks. This misses the smooth vol-sensitivity surface.

3. **Training sigma coverage vs Vega magnitude**: At T=2y, K=S, a 1% change in sigma yields ~5.6 price units (from BS approximation: Vega ≈ S·√T ≈ 200·1.41 = 14.1 per unit; ×0.5% = 7). The network must be very accurate in the sigma dimension to recover this.
