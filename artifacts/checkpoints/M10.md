## Objective

Probe the M6-trained surrogate's robustness outside its training distribution across three stress categories:

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Spot jumps | ±30 % (log-moneyness shifts by ±log 1.3 / log 0.7) |
| Vol spike | σ = 0.80 (training max was 0.60, ~33 % OOD) |
| Maturity extremes | T = 0.02 (near-expiry, training min 0.08); T = 3.5 (beyond training max 3.0) |

## Interpretation

**Strong regions:**
- `vol_spike_80pct`: perfect validity and monotonicity — the surrogate extrapolates cleanly in σ.
- `extreme_long_maturity` (T = 3.5): marginal OOD, near-perfect behaviour.
- Spot monotonicity (dP/dS < 0): ≥ 98.9 % in all scenarios — directional pricing is intact.

**Weak regions:**
- `near_expiry` (T = 0.02): 74.4 % non-negative and 62.2 % above-intrinsic — the surrogate
  struggles far below the training minimum T = 0.08. Rapid time-value collapse is hard to
  extrapolate with a smooth MLP.
- `spot_jump_up_30pct`: 78.9 % non-negative. Deep ITM base samples pushed to log-moneyness
  > 0.5 (OOD domain) produce negative prices; 100 % delta-sign pass confirms directional
  integrity is preserved even when price magnitudes are wrong.
- `spot_jump_down_30pct`: deep OTM extrapolation causes 15.6 % delta sign violations
  (delta goes slightly positive), consistent with ReLU kinks at the OOD boundary.
- Vol monotonicity at base (88.9 %): a small number of short-maturity OTM samples show
  near-zero vega, where the surrogate's finite resolution causes non-monotone steps.
