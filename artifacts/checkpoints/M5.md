## M5 method
1. Stratified sampling over 9 buckets:
   OTM/ATM/ITM x short/medium/long.
2. Weighted objective:
   - short_maturity_weight = 2.5
   - wing_moneyness_weight = 2.0
   - Applied multiplicatively when a sample is both short and wing.
